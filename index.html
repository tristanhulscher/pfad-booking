Questions

How do I know how many days I need?

Most single rooms or a batch of touch-ups fit in one day. After you book, send us photos and we'll tell you honestly if it needs more or less. If you're really unsure, call us first and we'll estimate it for you.

What about paint?

If you already have paint, great. If not, tell us your colours and we'll order it in advance so your painter shows up ready. Paint and primer are billed at cost on top of the day rate.

Do I need to be home?

Someone needs to let the painter in at the start of the day (or leave us a key). After that, you're free to go about your day.

When will my painter come?

We'll call to schedule you within 24 hours of booking (Mon–Fri, 8:30am–4:30pm) and pick a date that works for you.

What if my job takes less than a full day?

We'll suggest other small jobs to fill the rest of the day. If there's nothing else, the unused time becomes a credit toward a future Envision booking.

What if I need a little more time?

If your job just needs a little extra time to wrap up, we'll take care of it and only charge for the portion of a day used, at your discounted next-day rate. For example, 2 extra hours is a quarter of a day. We'll always check with you before going past your booked time.

Is the work guaranteed to be finished in a day?

You're booking a painter's full day, not a fixed scope. We'll get as much done as can be done well. If you need a guaranteed scope and our 10-year interior warranty, book a free estimate instead.

What if I need to reschedule or cancel?

Life happens! Bookings are non-refundable, but your money is never lost. Reschedule with at least 48 hours' notice, or if your job wraps up early, and any unused time becomes a credit toward a future Envision booking.
