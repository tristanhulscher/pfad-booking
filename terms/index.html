Painter For A Day terms

Envision Painting Ltd. · Last updated October 9, 2026

The short version

  • You're booking one Envision painter for a full 8-hour day, not a fixed job.
  • Paint and primer are extra, billed at cost if we pick them up for you.
  • Paid in full at booking. No refunds, but you never lose your money: reschedule for free, or cancel for a credit that never expires.
  • Painter For A Day work isn't covered by our warranty.

  1. The service

    Envision Painting Ltd. ("we") will provide one painter, who is an Envision employee, for 8 hours of on-site work per booked day. Work is booked and billed by the day, not by the task. Basic prep materials (patching compound and caulking for normal prep), tools, drop sheets and clean-up are included.

  2. Rates and payment

    Payment is due in full at the time of booking. Payments are non-refundable (see term 6). Regular rates, plus GST:

    Day 1$795
    Day 2 (booked with Day 1)$695
    Day 3 (booked with Days 1 and 2)$595

    Multi-day rates apply only to consecutive days booked together. Promotional prices apply only at the time of booking.

  3. Scheduling and hours

    We'll contact you within 24 hours (business days, Mon–Fri, 8:30am–4:30pm) to schedule your date. Work takes place on weekdays between 8am and 5pm.

  4. Paint and materials

    Paint and primer are not included. You can supply your own, or ask us to pick them up and we'll bill them at cost on top of the day rate. Time spent at the paint store or colour matching counts toward your 8 hours. Materials for heavy prep (for example a full day of caulking, or patching large holes in drywall) are extra.

  5. Access

    Someone must let your painter in at the start of the day, or arrange a key or lockbox in advance. You don't need to be home all day.

  6. No refunds, credits instead

    All payments are non-refundable, but your money is never lost. You can reschedule at no charge. You can also cancel at any time, and the full amount you paid becomes a credit toward a future Envision Painting booking. The same applies if your job is finished before your booked time is used: the unused amount becomes a credit.

    Credits never expire. A credit is worth the dollar amount you paid, not a number of days, so if our prices go up, your credit covers the amount you paid and you pay any difference. Credits are applied toward a future booking at the regular first-day rate, since multi-day pricing only applies to consecutive days booked together. Credits can be transferred to another person on the same terms.

  7. Scope and extra time

    Because we don't visit the job ahead of time, we can't guarantee how much will be completed in a day. Your painter will walk through the work with you at the start of the day and let you know what's realistic. If your job needs just a little more time to finish, we'll bill it as a portion of your next day's rate (the 2nd-day rate after a 1-day booking; the 3rd-day rate after 2 or more days). Each extra hour is 1/8 of that day rate, plus GST. We'll always get your approval before going past your booked time. Anything more is booked as an additional day.

  8. No warranty

    Painter For A Day work is not covered by our 5- and 10-year warranties. For warranty coverage and a guaranteed scope, book a free estimate at envisionpainting.ca.

  9. Moving items

    We're happy to move light furniture and items at your request, but we aren't liable for damage to items moved on your behalf. We don't remove door hardware, light fixtures, blinds or toilet tanks unless you ask.

  10. Property damage

    We take every precaution to avoid damage. If we accidentally cause damage, we will repair it or cover the reasonable cost. We are not liable for pre-existing weak or faulty areas of the property.

  11. Pets

    You're responsible for your pets during the work.

  12. Additional charges

    Any balance owing after your booking (for example paint we purchased for you, or approved extra time) is due within 30 days of invoice. Late balances accrue 2% interest per month.
